# NEXUS — Stock Adjustment Glossary Addendum

## Phase 0.2 Inventory Discovery — Checkpoint 0.2-A

### Stock Adjustment

A business document used to change current inventory quantity for an authorized and allowed business reason or originating operation.

### Adjustment Reason

The allowed business cause that explains why the Stock Adjustment is being created.

### Originating Reference

A reference to the business operation that caused or justifies the Stock Adjustment when such a reference exists.

### Issuance

The point at which the Stock Adjustment becomes effective and its inventory effect is applied immediately.

### Immutability

Once issued, the Stock Adjustment cannot be edited or cancelled.
