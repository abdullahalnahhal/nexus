BR-01
POS belongs to exactly one Branch.

BR-02
POS cannot be transferred between Branches.

BR-03
Warehouse belongs to exactly one Branch.

BR-04
Warehouse cannot be transferred between Branches.

BR-05
User may operate across multiple Branches.

BR-06
User capabilities depend on Role/Permissions.

BR-07
POS performs Sales and Returns only.

BR-08
POS does not perform Purchase operations.

BR-09
POS operates against its Branch Warehouse.

BR-10
A POS has its own Wallet.

BR-11
A POS may have multiple Shifts over time.

BR-12
A POS cannot start a new Shift before
the previous Shift is properly confirmed/settled.

BR-13
A Shift has exactly one Salesman.

BR-14
A Shift may have multiple Managers.

BR-15
A Salesman cannot be assigned to two POSes
simultaneously in the same shift context.

BR-16
A Manager may participate in multiple POSes
within the same shift.

BR-17
A Shift ending with Branch settlement
requires Branch Manager confirmation.

BR-18
Branch settlement increases Branch Wallet
and decreases POS Wallet by the same amount.

BR-19
A Shift may end using REMAIN instead of settlement.

BR-20
REMAIN requires confirmation by either
the incoming Manager or incoming Salesman.

BR-21
After REMAIN confirmation, the carried balance
continues into the next Shift's POS Wallet.
