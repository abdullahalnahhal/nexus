# NEXUS — Stock Adjustment Vision Addendum

## Phase 0.2 Inventory Discovery — Checkpoint 0.2-A

Stock Adjustment reinforces the NEXUS principle that inventory state is changed through controlled business operations rather than arbitrary direct edits.

```text
Allowed Reason / Origin
          ↓
Permission
          ↓
Stock Adjustment
          ↓
Immediate Inventory Effect
          ↓
Historical Traceability
```

The current phase deliberately keeps the document operational: no accounting effect, no UOM rules, and no additional approval workflow are introduced here.
