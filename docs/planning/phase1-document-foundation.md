# NEXUS — Phase 1 Document Foundation Planning

## Foundation Status

**Document Foundation: CLOSED**

## Scope Completed

- Shared Document identity and metadata.
- Document-specific lifecycle principle.
- Document status foundation.
- Document Line identity and Product reference.
- Document Reference model and immutability rules.
- Operational scope references.
- Issuance and cancellation metadata.
- User Activity vs Business History separation.
- Attachment infrastructure boundary.
- Company currency inheritance.
- Financial total fields and payment entries.
- Product reference/history principle.

## Explicit Reuse From Phase 0

The Foundation reuses existing decisions for Branch, Warehouse, POS, Product, Customer, Supplier, Inventory, Permissions, Stock Adjustment, Transfer, Returns, and other previously discovered business behavior.

## Deferred / Document-Specific

The following are intentionally not standardized here:

- Exact lifecycle states.
- Cancellation workflow shape.
- Reason catalogs.
- Business History structure.
- Attachment requirements.
- Notes/Comments.
- Financial formulas beyond the shared persisted fields.
- Payment-specific business rules.
