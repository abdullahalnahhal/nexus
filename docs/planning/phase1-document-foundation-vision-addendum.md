# NEXUS — Phase 1 Document Foundation Vision Addendum

## Foundation Principle

NEXUS Documents are business records with common technical foundations but different business lifecycles.

```text
Shared Foundation
      ↓
Document-specific Lifecycle
      ↓
Document-specific Business Rules
      ↓
Business Effect
```

## Important Boundaries

- Technical identity is always present.
- Business Reference Number is optional according to the Document type.
- Operational scope is explicit when relevant.
- User activity logging is separated from Domain-specific Business History.
- Financial totals and payment information are persisted only where the Document nature requires them.
- Product master information remains referenced rather than generically duplicated on Documents.

## Outcome

The Document Foundation provides a stable vocabulary for Phase 1 while preserving the domain-specific behavior already discovered in Phase 0.
