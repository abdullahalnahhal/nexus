# NEXUS — Stock Adjustment Discovery Planning Addendum

## Phase 0.2 Inventory Discovery — Checkpoint 0.2-A

### Scope completed

Stock Adjustment business discovery has been completed for the current checkpoint.

### Confirmed behavior

- Warehouse context follows the existing warehouse-access and Permission model.
- Adjustment is justified by an allowed reason and/or originating reference.
- Manual creation is allowed for an authorized user.
- Stock changes immediately on issuance.
- Repeated Product lines are allowed and aggregate for inventory effect.
- Attachments are not required.
- No additional approval is required.
- Issued adjustments cannot be edited or cancelled.
- No accounting effect is modeled.
- UOM is deferred.

### Explicitly deferred

- UOM and fractional/whole-unit rules.
- Cost accounting and valuation.
- Detailed management/configuration of the allowed-reason catalog.

### Discovery principle

Previously settled cross-cutting Inventory rules are not repeated as new discovery questions. They remain inherited constraints of this document.
